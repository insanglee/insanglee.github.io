Assignment 8 and Assignment 9 Dashboard: NYC Subway Ridership and Arrest Patterns

This dashboard was initially created for EPPS 6356 Assignment 8 and expanded for Assignment 9 with a reactive spatial visualization.

The purpose of the dashboard is to examine subway ridership and arrest patterns across selected New York City subway stations while accounting for differences in passenger volume.

Research Question

How do subway ridership and arrest patterns vary across stations and over time, and how do patterns change after accounting for passenger volume?

Data Sources
  • MTA Subway Hourly Ridership Data
  • NYPD Arrest Data
  • NYC Open Data Borough Boundaries
Prototype Scope

The dashboard uses 2024 data for nine selected high-ridership subway station complexes in Manhattan, Queens, and Brooklyn.

The final project is planned to expand the analysis to the full subway network and the 2020–2024 period.

Unit of Analysis Station × month

Monthly subway ridership and arrest measures are calculated for each selected station complex.

Arrest Measure

NYPD arrests were assigned to the nearest selected subway station when the arrest occurred within 500 meters of that station.

This approach allows arrest activity near subway stations to be compared with passenger volume.

Key Measures
  • Monthly subway ridership
  • Monthly arrest count
  • Arrests per 100,000 riders
  • Station complex
  • Borough
  • Month

The ridership-adjusted arrest rate is calculated as:

Arrests per 100,000 riders = (Monthly arrests / Monthly ridership) × 100,000 Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

  • Monthly time-series line charts
  • Station-level scatter plot
  • Ridership-adjusted bubble chart
  • Reactive Leaflet choropleth map
  • Reactive station-level comparison chart
  • Interactive station-level data table
Map Interaction

Users can:

  • Select a month
  • Select a borough
  • View updated ridership and arrest totals
  • View ridership-adjusted arrest rates on the map
  • Compare station-level arrest rates in the companion chart
  • View borough information through map labels and popups
